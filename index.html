    One Year Together

    A letter from my heart

    I made this with lots of love — I really hope you like it.

    I’m so happy that we’ve been together for one year now. I’m in love with you, and I always will be. I can’t wait to see you again — to see your smile, to look into your eyes.

    I chose this song for our video because it’s one of my favorites, and whenever you look at me, I feel loved, I feel calm… and I know you’re the one for me. The other song I sang for you is simply because I miss you so much. I’m crazy to see you again, to have the missing piece of my heart right here with me.

    Thank you for being such an amazing man, my partner, my best friend. Thank you for being so kind and loving with me. I’m truly happy to be yours, and I thank God for us every day. I prayed so much for the day I’d meet the love of my life — and here we are, so in love! ❤️

    I pray this is just the first of many wonderful years together.

    It took a lot of work — the music production, me singing, CJ playing, and Kauã editing — but every second was worth it, just for you.
